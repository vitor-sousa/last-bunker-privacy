Privacy Policy — English

This Privacy Policy explains how Last Bunker: Merge Survivor collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

This game uses third-party services including Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob, and Google Play Billing. These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

1. Information we may collect

The app may collect or process the following types of data:

  • Game usage data such as screens visited, progress, match events, and interactions.
  • Device technical information such as model, operating system, language, region, and device identifiers.
  • Ad data such as impressions, clicks, rewarded ads, and measurement signals.
  • In-app purchase information such as purchased product, transaction status, and receipts needed for validation.
  • Local progress data such as Cores, Shards, unlocked heroes, upgrades, records, and daily rewards.

2. Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players use the app, which screens are visited, which events occur in the game, and how to improve balance, experience, and performance.

Events may include mission start and end, hero selection, SUPER usage, purchased upgrades, ads watched, purchase attempts, and completed purchases.

3. Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display ads in the app, including rewarded and interstitial ads. AdMob may use advertising identifiers, device data, approximate location, ad interactions, and other signals to serve, limit, measure, and improve ads.

Depending on your region and device settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

4. In-app purchases

The game offers in-app purchases such as Cores bundles, Hero Shards, and special packs. Transactions are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store full credit card data.

We may receive information necessary to confirm the purchase, deliver the digital item, and prevent fraud, such as product ID, purchase status, and transaction token/receipt.

5. How we use information

  • To operate and improve the game.
  • To save progress and rewards.
  • To show ads and reward ad-based interactions.
  • To process in-app purchases.
  • To analyze performance, retention, errors, and balance.
  • To prevent abuse, fraud, or reward manipulation.

6. Data sharing

We may share data with service providers required for app functionality, including Google Firebase, Google AdMob, and Google Play Billing. We do not sell your personal data directly.

7. Children

The app is not intentionally directed at children under 13 years old. If you believe a child has provided personal data improperly, contact us so we can review and remove the information when appropriate.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect app data. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is 100% secure.

9. Your rights and choices

You may limit ad tracking in your device settings, reset the advertising ID, and manage app permissions. You may also request information or deletion of data by contacting us.
